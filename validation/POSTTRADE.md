# Post-trade scoring

Source: `validation/data/okx.jsonl` (15 minutes of OKX BTC-USDT-SWAP, 818 books, 19,027 trades). 14 non-overlapping windows of 60 s, both sides, orders of $250,000, $2,500,000, $10,000,000. Generated by `python -m validation.posttrade`; the method, and what it cannot say, is in that module's docstring.

**Passive-limit sections withdrawn.** The scorer that produced them counted any trade printing through the touch as a full fill, whatever the order's size, so the tape "filled" 0.643 of a $250k order and of a $10M order alike. The scorer now counts a print through the price for its own size only. The passive sections return when the recording is re-scored; the TWAP and one-clip sections do not depend on that rule and are unchanged.

A positive refill error means the book had not refilled between slices as the schedule assumes.
